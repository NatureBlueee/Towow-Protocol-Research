## 为什么选它

A bilateral development relation proposed rules with public externalities; it reveals when parties to a contract are not the only entities with legitimate standing.

## 初始关系框架

- **frame_scope**：relation_with_public_institutional_constraints
- **R**：Waterfront Toronto, Sidewalk Labs, three levels of government, expert panel, public/affected communities, future development partners.
- **V**：Master plan, land development, digital proposals, data governance, public benefit, IP, approvals.
- **T**：Proposal → consultation/evaluation → threshold resolution → board/government approvals or refusal → implementation agreements or termination.
- **A**：Waterfront Toronto and Sidewalk approvals; public-sector/government authority over privacy, digital governance and land/public-interest decisions.
- **E**：Public consultation, technical evaluation, expert review, legal/regulatory compliance and future project readbacks.
- **D**：Collection, localization, governance, use and public value of urban data; IP and co-created assets.
- **O**：Pursue all/some/none; staged approvals; implementation agreements; withdrawal/termination.

## 权威拓扑

- **Waterfront Toronto Board/management**：evaluate public interest；lead government/privacy discussions；approve or reject plan
- **Sidewalk Labs corporate authority**：propose plan；accept threshold terms；fund/develop subject to agreements；withdraw under contract
- **governments and regulators**：law, land, planning, privacy and regulatory approvals
- **public and affected communities**：standing to challenge and shape public-interest evaluation; not identical to bilateral signature authority
- **DSAP**：independent expert challenge/advice; no final approval

## Operation → Effect → Adoption → Acceptance

- **operation**：Extensive plan development, consultation and threshold negotiation occurred; no full project implementation.
- **effect**：Formal governance/scope terms changed and the development agreement was terminated.
- **adoption**：Waterfront Toronto reports retaining planning insights; the Quayside proposal itself was not adopted.
- **acceptance**：No final multi-authority acceptance of the project. Public/expert positions remained heterogeneous.

## 本案支持什么

- Material relation change can be driven by authority, standing, data rights and effect rules rather than price or scope alone.
- Bilateral recognition is insufficient when third parties bear public externalities or possess public-law authority.
- Independent challenge can produce a real Design Delta without producing a final deal.
- A valid negative disposition and exit path are part of a truthful coordination system.

## 本案不支持什么

- It does not show that Towow or Agent mediation would have improved the project.
- It does not show that governance conflict caused termination.
- It does not establish the private preferences or complete internal worlds of either party.

## 竞争解释

- The threshold changes could be ordinary public procurement/governance correction rather than evidence for a new A2A-specific mechanism.
- The project may have remained commercially infeasible regardless of governance improvements.
- Published consultation records overrepresent views that entered official channels.

## 对系统设计的直接修改

- Add affected-party standing and representation to R/A; signature authority and legitimate challenge rights are distinct.
- Compile readiness for public-impact relations must include a legitimacy/standing closure, not only bilateral Mandates.
- Do not infer causal failure from a material unresolved issue when the recorded termination cause is different.
- Exit can preserve selected rights/knowledge; WITHDRAWN is not equivalent to erasing the relation history.

## 按时间顺序重建的诊断

- 前态中可见的 material 缺口：public-sector authority；data governance and localization；standing/consultation；IP/public-benefit evidence
- 后续实际变化：Threshold resolution changed the same authority/data/scope dimensions; this is chronology-consistent but not a blinded prediction.
- Gate 解释：A v0.4-style compile gate should have remained closed before threshold resolution and again where DSAP identified unresolved material details.

这不是盲化预测。编码者已知结局，因此它只能检验时间顺序和诊断一致性，不能作为预测准确率。
