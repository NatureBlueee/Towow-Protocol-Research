## 为什么选它

A higher-level mandate requires coordination but deliberately leaves local agencies discretion over how to coordinate. It directly tests whether one mechanism fits all sovereign domains.

## 初始关系框架

- **frame_scope**：institutional_mandate_with_local_relation_frames
- **R**：State mandate, groundwater sustainability agencies, local governments, water users, disadvantaged communities, tribes, private pumpers and basin-level collaborations.
- **V**：GSA/GSP, basin, sustainability objectives, data/models, JPA, MOU, coordination mechanisms and representation.
- **T**：State mandate → local agency formation → mechanism selection → plan coordination → implementation/monitoring and revision.
- **A**：State sets outcomes and deadlines; local agencies retain legal/autonomy interests and choose arrangements; participation and decision authority may differ.
- **E**：Plans, institutional agreements, basin data/models, interviews/observation and coordinated-outcome measures.
- **D**：Local groundwater data/model collection and sharing across agencies and affected users.
- **O**：Multiple mandated coordinated outcomes rather than a single universal organizational form.

## 权威拓扑

- **California state**：mandate sustainability planning and coordinated outcomes；deadlines and statutory constraints
- **local agencies/GSAs**：choose organizational and coordination mechanisms；manage local resources and plans
- **multi-agency JPAs/MOUs**：joint planning/decision rights as delegated by members
- **affected users and underrepresented communities**：stakeholder standing and knowledge; participation may not equal formal decision authority
- **researchers/facilitators**：observe, analyze and support; no substantive water authority

## Operation → Effect → Adoption → Acceptance

- **operation**：Agencies formed governance bodies, negotiated coordination mechanisms, shared data/models and developed basin plans.
- **effect**：Coordinated planning outputs and institutional arrangements were produced across basins.
- **adoption**：Local agencies adopted heterogeneous mechanisms under a common mandate.
- **acceptance**：Formal compliance and coordinated outcomes are observable; full stakeholder legitimacy/acceptance, especially for underrepresented groups, remains uneven and not reducible to plan adoption.

## 本案支持什么

- Non-collapsible authority does not imply one decentralized mechanism; autonomy concerns help determine which mechanism is viable.
- A common objective can coexist with heterogeneous local relation frames.
- Participation/standing and formal decision authority must be represented separately.
- Collaborative arrangements matter, but their configuration is outcome-contingent.

## 本案不支持什么

- It does not test Agent entities, digital Mandates or automated coordination.
- It does not establish that all local autonomy claims are normatively justified.
- Published configurational relationships are not randomized causal effects.

## 竞争解释

- Hydrology, prior relationships, basin size and resource conditions may explain both mechanism choice and outcomes.
- Top-down deadlines and legal sanctions may drive coordination more than voluntary formation.
- Interview and ethnographic interpretation may reflect access and respondent selection.

## 对系统设计的直接修改

- CollapseSafe routing must model autonomy/authority concerns, not only privacy and technical complexity.
- Relation Schema should support an institutional frame plus local relation-specific specializations without duplicating all objects.
- Add standing/representation coverage to compile readiness and evidence ledgers.
- Mechanism choice should be contingent: MOU, JPA, shared standard, central service or local Oracle are alternatives, not maturity levels.

## 按时间顺序重建的诊断

- 前态中可见的 material 缺口：which authority would be pooled；which coordination mechanism fits autonomy concerns；how data/models become comparable；how non-authority stakeholders are represented
- 后续实际变化：Basins adopted varied structures and collaborative configurations rather than converging on one organizational form.
- Gate 解释：A single centralized platform should not be assumed; the correct gate asks whether a chosen mechanism preserves required local authority while satisfying coordinated outcomes.

这不是盲化预测。编码者已知结局，因此它只能检验时间顺序和诊断一致性，不能作为预测准确率。
