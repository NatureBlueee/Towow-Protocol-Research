# Round 02 — Allbuddy principal

Action: `COUNTER`

Allbuddy accepted separation between execution claim, product adoption and
World presentation, but rejected premature receipt, cryptography and universal
freshness choices.

## Authority answers

- No canonical execution issuer exists yet: `workspace-runtime` `completed`
  is still an unauthenticated local adapter claim.
- The independent Acceptance issuer must be an Allbuddy-controlled product
  consumer/adoption authority, separate from the executor. The in-browser
  `ProductShell.finalizeRun` state is not stable or readable enough.
- Keying, rotation, revocation and policy/version remain `UNKNOWN`; they must
  fail closed until issuer and threat model exist.
- A universal 15-minute TTL and five-minute clock skew were rejected. Validity
  belongs to the Allbuddy status policy and may vary for offline or
  human-adoption work.
- Existence is default-deny. Only tenant-authorized, viewer-scoped,
  non-enumerable references may disclose coarse status.
- Corrections, disputes and refusal remain append-only coarse states. World
  cannot see reason text or adjudicate adoption.

## Removed over-prescription

- Anonymous action/scope were not accepted because they may still fingerprint
  enterprise activity.
- Payload commitments were not accepted because low-entropy private facts can
  remain guessable even through hashes.
- HMAC was not selected because sharing verification material expands
  authority and does not by itself establish independence.
- A Viewer verdict may only mean that a named Allbuddy Acceptance authority
  made a status decision; it cannot mean that the answer was correct or the
  execution effect occurred.

## Minimum semantic boundary

Non-replayable, non-enumerable, viewer-scoped reference; explicit separation of
execution and adoption; verifiable issuer and policy; status-specific time
boundary; correction/revocation relation; and a content-free
redaction/classification declaration.

Missing issuer/adoption, expiry, revocation, signature anomaly or reference
misuse must remain `UNKNOWN/UNVERIFIABLE`, never fall back to producer `PASS`.

Formation had not yet occurred at this point; World still needed to accept the
reduced authority and privacy boundary.
