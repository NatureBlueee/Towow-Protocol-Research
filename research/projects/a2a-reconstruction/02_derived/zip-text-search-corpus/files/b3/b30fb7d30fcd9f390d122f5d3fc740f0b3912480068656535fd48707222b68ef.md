# Model agents

## Experimental subjects

All Claude subjects used Claude Code CLI 2.1.219 with model alias `sonnet`.
Provider traces report `claude-sonnet-5` and internal
`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` routing. Calls were tool-less, non-persistent,
schema-constrained, and received prompts on stdin.

- Source owner A:
  [private dossier](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/inputs/agent_a_private.md),
  [round prompts](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/prompts/).
- Source owner B:
  [private dossier](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/inputs/agent_b_private.md),
  [round prompts](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/prompts/).
- Static builder:
  [prompt](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/prompts/static_builder.md).
- Central owner reporters and builder:
  [prompts](../experiments/r5-4-run-001-source-identity/prompts/).

Every call has `request.json`, raw `stdout.json`, `stderr.txt`, and normalized
`parsed.json`. Costs are recomputed in
[MODEL_COSTS.json](../experiments/MODEL_COSTS.json).

## Research assistants, not experimental subjects

Two Codex sub-agents performed read-only independent reviews:

- blind mechanism reviewer: no private dossier, evaluator, oracle, other
  worktree, or Git ref;
- oracle-aware engineering reviewer: frozen outputs plus hidden branch, with
  permission to reject the expected conclusion.

Their outputs are under [independent_reviews](../independent_reviews/).

Task-selection and isolation scouts were used before the experiment. They did
not participate as owner subjects and are not counted as model calls in the
architecture comparison.
