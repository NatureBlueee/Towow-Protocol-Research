## 为什么选它

Many locally contracted outputs had to become one operational railway. The case exposes the gap between package completion, system integration, assurance and entry into service.

## Operation → Effect → Adoption → Acceptance

- **operation**：Hundreds of design, construction, systems, test and handover operations were performed across packages.
- **effect**：A safe, integrated railway capable of carrying passengers—not package completion—was the decisive target-world Effect.
- **adoption**：Operational organizations took over and the Elizabeth line entered service.
- **acceptance**：Entry into service and sponsor/regulator approvals provide operational acceptance; broader benefit realization is a separate long-run question.

## 本案支持什么

- ActionAttempt/package output is not equivalent to integrated system Effect.
- A formal integration schema can exist while enacted authority, evidence and incentives fail.
- Compile readiness requires end-to-end outcome witnesses and dependency-realistic evidence, not optimistic local progress.
- Reopen can be triggered by assurance and governance failure, not only technical version change.

## 本案不支持什么

- It does not involve autonomous AI Agents or private digital worlds.
- It does not prove that a different information architecture alone would have prevented delay.
- Retrospective lessons do not identify a single cause of a complex programme failure.

## 竞争解释

- Fixed-date political/sponsor pressure, commercial incentives, construction uncertainty and organizational culture may independently explain failure.
- The integration plan may have been adequate on paper but under-resourced or weakly enforced.
- Eventual delivery may reflect extraordinary additional resources rather than superior governance alone.

## 对系统设计的直接修改

- Separate declared schema from enacted schema: authority and witness rules must have observable enforcement, not only documentation.
- Operation/Effect/Adoption/Acceptance must be end-to-end and target-world specific.
- Compile readiness needs dependency realism, integrated assurance and incentive alignment.
- Add assurance failure, reporting opacity and objective drift to Defeater/reopen triggers.

## 按时间顺序重建的诊断

- 前态中可见的 material 缺口：interdependency-realistic plan；integrated technical/programmatic assurance；effective sponsor levers；interface-accountability alignment
- 后续实际变化：Delay and reviews led to reset, stronger oversight and revised integration/readiness practices.
- Gate 解释：A gate based on package progress would pass incorrectly; an end-to-end Effect gate would remain closed.

这不是盲化预测。编码者已知结局，因此它只能检验时间顺序和诊断一致性，不能作为预测准确率。

## 仍然未知

- Exact counterfactual cost of alternative contract/interface allocation
- Private board/executive deliberations
- Causal weights among schedule, culture, incentives and technical complexity
