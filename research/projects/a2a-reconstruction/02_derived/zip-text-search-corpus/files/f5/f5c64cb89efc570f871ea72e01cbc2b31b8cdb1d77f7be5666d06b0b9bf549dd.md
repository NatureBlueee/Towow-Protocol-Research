## Research conclusions

1. `ActionAttempt`, `Effect`, `Verification`, and `Acceptance` must remain
   semantically distinguishable. The naive terminal baseline chose a wrong
   classification or recovery in 10 of 17 scenarios.
2. Four parallel authoritative objects are not supported by this evidence.
   Existing Harness events, carrier files, sentinels, indexes, and fresh
   readback matched the explicit four-stage reconstruction in 17 of 17
   scenarios.
3. `EscalationAnswerApplied(resumed=true)` can be emitted when an answer is
   durably parked for future redispatch, before a fresh session consumes it.
   The current event wording therefore combines accepted-for-redispatch with
   verified consumption.
4. The preregistered six-axis Capability Claim failed its threshold: strict
   first-attempt accuracy was 4/6 (66.7%), below 80%. It outperformed the static
   installed baseline at 3/6, but omitted concrete filesystem formation
   conditions.
5. For this local task, least-privilege central execution plus existing Harness
   truth sources is the best-supported baseline. Sovereign coordination
   superiority was not demonstrated and must remain an open claim.
