# r5-run-001-evidence-reachability

## Research question

Can the baseline claim matrix in the v1.1 packet be followed to the raw local
artifacts needed to independently inspect or reproduce those claims?

## Competing explanations

1. The packet contains enough local artifacts for claim-level audit.
2. The packet is intentionally a seed/summary packet, so most claims are source
   statements whose raw evidence lives elsewhere.
3. Paths are present but resolve from a different documented root.

## Decision impact

If raw artifacts are missing, packet checksum success establishes transport
integrity only. Imported claims must remain source statements in this local
round and cannot be promoted by repeating their prose.

## Reality and limits

This is an E4 audit of the actual local packet, but it tests evidence
reachability rather than the empirical truth of the baseline claims.
