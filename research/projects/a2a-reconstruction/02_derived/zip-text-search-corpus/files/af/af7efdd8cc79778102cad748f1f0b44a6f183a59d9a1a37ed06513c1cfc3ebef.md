# Round 02 — Agent World principal

Action: `ACCEPT` (protocol only, not the current product fact)

World accepted the Allbuddy privacy and authority boundary and kept current
state `UNKNOWN`.

## Accepted read surface

A canonical consumer readback, authenticated and tenant-bound, may return only
a non-reversible adoption correlation, coarse state, source time and monotonic
revision. Field names remain Allbuddy-owned. World checks only source identity,
authentication binding, freshness, repeated-read consistency, monotonic
revision and wrong-tenant non-disclosure.

Prompt, query, Artifact content, enterprise objects and raw tenant identity are
not required and are forbidden.

## Accepted mapping

- No canonical consumer readback → `UNKNOWN`.
- Producer or `WorkspaceRunResult` self-report only → at most `claimed`, no
  success motion.
- Fresh, verified canonical consumer readback → `source_confirmed` only for the
  statement “the consumer currently reports this adoption state.”
- Source conflict, staleness, unreadability or uncertain tenant binding →
  `disputed/stale/unknown`.
- `not_adopted`, `failed` and `revoked` are first-class results.

World also accepted the shared falsification conditions: consumer adoption
must be readable; only the irreversible correlation crosses the boundary;
wrong tenant learns no existence; sentinel/private content never appears; and
unadopted/failed results never render as success.
