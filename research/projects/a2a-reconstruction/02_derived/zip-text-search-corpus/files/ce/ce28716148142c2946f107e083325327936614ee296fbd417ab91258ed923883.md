# Blind mechanism review

Reviewer: independent Codex sub-agent, read-only, oracle-blind  
Scope: preregistration, public/static/central/A2A traces only  
Verdict: **Reject the strong claim that direct A2A formed a new possibility or
completed a success. Accept only that it formed an unclosed actionable joint
condition candidate.**

## Increment over baselines

- The static builder already found composite source/locator identity and covered
  the main projection, invalidation, auxiliary-event, and migration surfaces.
- Central owner B independently required source-less state to be a distinct
  legacy identity rather than a wildcard; therefore this core rule was not an
  A2A-unique discovery.
- Central owner A required historical global-removal compatibility. The fixed
  central query therefore exposed the same A/B value conflict, although its
  builder call ended with a connection closure.
- Direct A2A advanced the conflict into a near-joint candidate: absent source is
  a non-wildcard historical class, future removals must carry source, and
  supersede/auxiliary obligations must be owner-specific. Later rounds also
  introduced severability, infeasibility disclosure, dispute rights, and an
  entitlement floor.

## Classification

- Composite identity: discovery/confirmation; also present in static and
  central outputs.
- Null-source-class/no-wildcard: independently discovered by B, then became a
  joint-condition candidate when A adopted it in round 3.
- Lapse, severability, entitlement floor, and disclosure: interaction-formed
  governance conditions, still unclosed at round 6.
- The negotiation did not merely restate language and did not lower B's goal:
  B repeatedly refused to waive the underlying supersede-response and
  single-instance-routing entitlement.
- No operational world change was established.
