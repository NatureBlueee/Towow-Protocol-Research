# Analysis

The replication falsified the one-off-failure hypothesis: a byte-identical
prompt again closed mid-response at about five minutes. This is reproducible
runtime/transport evidence for this invocation shape, not evidence that a
least-privilege coordinator cannot reason about the conflict.

The two owner reports already exposed both incompatible legacy policies. A
shorter or streaming central synthesis could plausibly succeed, but changing
the prompt or output surface after observing two failures would be a new
protocol. It is left as the next discriminating action rather than silently
used to rescue this baseline.
