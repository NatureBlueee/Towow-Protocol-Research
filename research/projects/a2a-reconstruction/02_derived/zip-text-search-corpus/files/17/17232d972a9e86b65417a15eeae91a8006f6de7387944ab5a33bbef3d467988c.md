## 初始关系框架

- **frame_scope**：institutional_market_frame
- **R**：CMA, CMA9 banks, OBIE, third-party providers, consumers/SMEs, FCA, PSR, Treasury and future standards body.
- **V**：API standards, participants, directory, read/write data, service levels, disputes, breaches, suspension/exclusion.
- **T**：Market remedy → standards/governance implementation → participant registration/runtime → monitoring/review → roadmap completion → transition.
- **A**：CMA Order and oversight, OBIE implementation authority, bank/provider obligations, later regulatory allocation to FCA/PSR/Treasury.
- **E**：Conformance, directory status, service monitoring, investigation, governance review and roadmap decisions.
- **D**：Consumer/SME-authorized financial data sharing and payment/data-service rules.
- **O**：Operational participation, roadmap completion, breach/dispute handling, transition to sustainable framework.

## 权威拓扑

- **CMA**：impose remedy/order；oversee compliance；decide roadmap completion
- **OBIE governance**：develop/operate standards and ecosystem under Order
- **CMA9 and participants**：implement interfaces and comply with rules
- **FCA/PSR/Treasury**：future regulatory supervision and smart-data framework
- **consumers/SMEs**：authorize data access/use within the regulated frame; not ecosystem governance principals

## Operation → Effect → Adoption → Acceptance

- **operation**：Banks/providers implemented and operated standardized APIs and directory/governance processes.
- **effect**：Data-sharing/payment capabilities became available at scale.
- **adoption**：Millions of users and hundreds of providers used the ecosystem by the 2022 review.
- **acceptance**：Market adoption was substantial, while governance legitimacy and long-term institutional acceptance remained subject to review and transition.

## 本案支持什么

- Technical Effect and Adoption can coexist with institutional governance failure.
- Compilation includes standards, registration, dispute, suspension, monitoring and amendment—not only API generation.
- A temporary formation authority may need to be replaced by durable regulators after the relation stabilizes.
- Governance/authority defects are legitimate reopen triggers even when technical interfaces continue to work.

## 本案不支持什么

- The case does not test decentralized Agent-to-Agent interaction.
- It does not show that a Towow formation process would have prevented governance failures.
- User counts do not prove every participant accepted the governance model.

## 竞争解释

- Open Banking is a state-imposed market remedy; its formation dynamics may not transfer to voluntary non-standard relations.
- Reported adoption may be explained by mandate and bank scale rather than governance design.
- The lessons reviews are retrospective and institutionally authored.

## 对系统设计的直接修改

- Represent stability as a vector: technical/operational stability cannot average away authority/governance instability.
- Define governance debt as a diagnostic when technical runtime is compiled before oversight, dispute and end-state authority are settled; do not create a new aggregate root.
- Compile outputs must include amendment, suspension, complaint, dispute, monitoring and institutional succession.
- Reopen may target governance subgraphs while leaving safe operational subgraphs running.

## 按时间顺序重建的诊断

- 前态中可见的 material 缺口：scale/complexity estimation；durable oversight；checks on implementation authority；formal review/escalation gates
- 后续实际变化：The 2021–2023 reviews and transition addressed those governance/end-state dimensions.
- Gate 解释：Technical compile readiness alone would have produced a false sense of closure; authority/governance stability needed an independent gate.

这不是盲化预测。编码者已知结局，因此它只能检验时间顺序和诊断一致性，不能作为预测准确率。

## 仍然未知

- User-level informed acceptance
