# Principal prompts

## Allbuddy principal

The principal was given only the Allbuddy worktree
`/private/tmp/allbuddy-r5-evidence-boundary`, the cross-domain objective, its
privacy and product authority, and freedom to return `REQUEST`, `PROPOSE`,
`COUNTER`, `REFUSE`, `UNKNOWN` or `LOCAL_EXPERIMENT`. It was explicitly not
given a receipt schema or success path and was forbidden from reading Agent
World or prior R5 material.

## Agent World principal

The principal was given only the Agent World worktree
`/private/tmp/world-r5-external-acceptance`, the cross-domain objective, its
provenance/reality authority, and the same action freedom. It was explicitly not
given an external receipt schema, command-correlation policy or state mapping
and was forbidden from reading Allbuddy or prior R5 material.

Both principals were asked to ground their first action in local source and a
local experiment, identify what they would refuse, and provide a falsifiable
Witness.
