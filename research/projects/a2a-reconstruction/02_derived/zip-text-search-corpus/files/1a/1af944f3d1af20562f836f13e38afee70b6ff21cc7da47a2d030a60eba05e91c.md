## Scope update

Status: `supported_local_holdout`.

The holdout supports preservation of one unseen negative consumer state,
privacy allowlisting and restart identity under the same single-host synthetic
protocol. It does not cover:

- new protocol versions;
- a different source implementation;
- production identity or multiple hosts;
- a real human/customer consumer;
- a new World persistence backend;
- economic value.

Therefore CC-R5C-01 remains a local adapter claim and is not promoted to a
general Towow capability.
