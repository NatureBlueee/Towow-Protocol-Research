# 附录 H　主要技术与方法参考

1. A2A Project, *Agent2Agent Protocol Specification*: independent Agent discovery, messages, tasks and artifacts.
2. Model Context Protocol, *Authorization Specification*: OAuth 2.1 protected resource access for MCP clients and servers.
3. W3C, *Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0*: issuer-holder-verifier claims and tamper-evident credentials.
4. SPIFFE/SPIRE: attested workload identity for heterogeneous infrastructure.
5. Cedar Policy Language and OpenFGA: policy validation and relationship-based authorization adapters.
6. CloudEvents: common event envelope; Temporal: durable workflow and event history; OpenTelemetry: traces, metrics and logs.
7. S. D. Sarasvathy, “Causation and Effectuation,” *Academy of Management Review*, 2001.
8. T. Baker and R. E. Nelson, “Creating Something from Nothing: Resource Construction through Entrepreneurial Bricolage,” *Administrative Science Quarterly*, 2005.
9. K. Muszyński et al., OECD SME and Entrepreneurship Papers no. 45, 2023: interviews on online freelancers and platform power asymmetry.
