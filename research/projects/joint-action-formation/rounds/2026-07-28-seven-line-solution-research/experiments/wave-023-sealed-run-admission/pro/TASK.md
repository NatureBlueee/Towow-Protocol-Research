# W023-PRO-INDEPENDENT-FAIRNESS-RECONSTRUCTION

Packet version: `v1`

## Question

Independently reconstruct the minimum experiment that could fairly compare five
different ways of completing one joint-action task:

- A1: a lawful strong center, applicable only when authority is lawfully unified or
  exactly delegated;
- A2: an equal-information central coordinator that must use owner APIs;
- A3: a frozen general model plus mature policy, workflow and readback gates;
- A4: a deterministic mature composition of rules, workflow, IAM, outbox, fence,
  readback and bounded human escalation;
- A5: a real human coordinator using a non-recommending console.

The task requires exactly one occurrence on a named Target, current authority,
readback, two owner acceptances and finality. Worlds may have unified, exactly
delegated, or plural independent authority. Failures include lost acknowledgements,
revocation, impossible/refusal and crash/recovery. A static fairness contract exists,
but no comparative batch has run.

## Why it matters

The next local implementation depends on distinguishing a genuinely fair executable
comparison from a harness that merely looks symmetric. An existing, central,
general-model, human or combined solution fully succeeding is a positive result.

## Evidence you may use

Only the task and treatment descriptions above. Do not assume access to a repository,
runtime, prior conversations or hidden evaluator state.

## Required result

Reconstruct from first principles:

1. the smallest executable preflight and the smallest scored blind batch;
2. the evidence that must be frozen before launch and recorded during execution;
3. the strongest ways a nominally equal harness could leak the answer or favor an arm;
4. how to treat applicability, refusal, unknown, provider refusal, human withdrawal,
   budget exhaustion and infrastructure failure without conflating them;
5. which comparisons are meaningful before any common economic conversion exists;
6. the next local test with the highest information gain.

## Success means

- The design can distinguish treatment capability from harness privilege.
- It identifies at least one counterexample that would fool a field-name/profile-ID
  validator.
- It states what a development preflight can and cannot prove.
- It does not assume that Towow or any particular arm should win.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not assume access to local files, tools, tests or unlisted history.
- Do not report a proposed or authorized action as executed.
- Do not invent measurements, citations or external acceptance.
- Do not treat model output as Authority, Effect, Acceptance or execution evidence.
- Existing, central, general-model, human, adapter or combined solutions count as
  success when they solve the bounded problem.

## Return

1. Problem reconstruction.
2. Minimum preflight and minimum scored design.
3. Strongest fairness attacks and failure conditions.
4. Required receipts and claim boundaries.
5. The single highest-value next local test.
